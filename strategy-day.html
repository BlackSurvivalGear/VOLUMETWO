01 / Why a Strategy Day

Make space for the
decisions that matter.

Good strategy needs room to breathe.

The V2 Strategy Day creates a focused space to step back from operational pressure, examine what is really happening and decide what needs to happen next.

It is built around your business rather than a fixed template, bringing strategy, commercial thinking and creativity into one working session.

02 / What we can work on

One day.
Clearer priorities.

The focus is shaped around the challenge you bring. Depending on what the business needs, a Strategy Day can help you work through areas such as:

01

Sales strategy

Clarify your route to market, target opportunities and the actions needed to create stronger commercial momentum.

02

Business roadmap

Turn an ambition into a practical roadmap that can support growth, funding or investment conversations.

03

Brand & positioning

Strengthen how the business is positioned and how its value is understood in the marketplace.

04

Marketing & social

Review activity, identify opportunities and shape campaigns or communications around a clear commercial purpose.

05

New opportunities

Explore partnerships, propositions or routes to growth that could open up the next stage of the business.

06

A challenge of your own

Bring the issue that needs attention. We will shape the day around the context, ambition and decisions in front of you.
